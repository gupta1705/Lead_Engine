Privacy policy

TVF Lead Engine · last updated 14 October 2026

TVF Lead Engine is an internal tool that The Viral Fever (TVF) and its LCX team use to find companies, write business emails to them and send the emails a team member has approved. Only people TVF has given a login can use it. This page explains what the tool does with a team member's Google account when they connect their Gmail.

What connecting your Gmail allows

When you connect your Gmail (account menu, Your Gmail), Google asks you to allow the tool to read, compose and send email for that one address. The tool uses that permission only to:

  • send, from your address, the emails you have approved in the tool;
  • read the replies in the threads of those emails, so the team can see who answered;
  • read your Gmail address, so the emails you approve go out from the right account.

It does not read, keep or use any other email in your mailbox.

What is stored

  • Google's sign-in for your address, kept encrypted, so the tool can send without asking you to sign in each time. Your Google password is never seen or kept.
  • The emails sent through the tool and the replies to them (addresses, subject, text and dates), as the team's record of who was contacted and what they said.

Who else handles it

  • The tool runs on Render and keeps its records in a Supabase database.
  • The text of a reply may be passed to Anthropic's Claude API only to sort it as interested, not interested or an automatic reply.

Gmail data is not sold, not shared for advertising and not used to train AI models. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time from the tool (account menu, Your Gmail, Disconnect), which deletes the stored sign-in and withdraws Google's permission, or from your Google account's security settings. Emails already sent stay in the team's records.

Questions

Ask the TVF team member who gave you your login, or write to the support address shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
