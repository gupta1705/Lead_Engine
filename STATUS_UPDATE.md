## What the engine will do

Instead of manually searching for companies one by one, the engine will **automatically scrape and pull leads from the internet** — company websites, YouTube, industry directories, and more — then check each one against our criteria, find the right contact person, verify their email, and score how good a fit they are. We only review and approve the final shortlist.
